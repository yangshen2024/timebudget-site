Privacy Policy

TimeBudget · Effective: 2026-04-22 · Last updated: 2026-04-29

TL;DR: TimeBudget collects nothing. Everything you enter (salary, wishlist, etc.) stays on your device. No servers, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.

This app ("TimeBudget", hereafter "the app") is developed and distributed by YANG SHEN (Apple Developer Team ID 27GPV267N2) as an individual developer. It helps users convert prices into working hours for rational spending decisions.

1. What we collect

1.1 The app does not actively collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

1.2 Data you voluntarily enter in Settings:

  • Country / industry / position / years of experience
  • Monthly salary, daily working hours, paid days per month
  • Optional custom tax rate
  • Wishlist items (name / price / currency)

1.3 All such data is stored only in this device's local storage (browser localStorage / iOS app sandbox). It never leaves your device.

2. How we use it

Only to instantly compute and display "hourly wage", "time needed", "after-tax estimate", "share card", etc. on this device. It is never used for ads, profiling, ML training, or any commercial distribution.

3. Sharing & disclosure

We never share, sell, rent, or disclose your data to any third party. There is no third-party server receiving your data because we do not make outgoing network requests at runtime.

4. Third-party SDKs

None. No analytics, ads, push, location, payment, or social-login SDKs are integrated.

5. Permissions

On iOS, the app requests no system permissions (no camera, no location, no contacts, no microphone, no tracking).

On Android, only the INTERNET permission is declared by default (needed by the WebView runtime for loading local offline assets). The app does not make runtime network requests.

6. Security & retention

Data lives only in your device's local storage, protected by your OS and device security. Uninstalling the app clears it automatically. You can also clear data manually via "Clear wishlist" / "Reset to defaults" in Settings.

7. Children

The app is not targeted at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Policy changes

If any future version adds network features or integrates a third-party SDK, we will notify you in-app and request consent again. You may always decline and stop using the app.

9. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Clear all in-app data in Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local storage
  • Email us to ask questions or exercise your rights
