Privacy Policy — Order: Priority

Effective: 19 May 2026 · Last updated: 19 May 2026

Plain-English summary: Order does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter — pillars, goals, tasks, time logs, abandoned items, reasons, reviews — stays on your device. We do not have servers. We do not use analytics SDKs. We do not show ads. We do not have a login system.

1. Data We Collect

None. The Order application performs zero network requests. All data is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We — the developers — have no access to it, and neither does any third party.

2. Third-Party Services

Order does not integrate any third-party SDK, including but not limited to:

  • No analytics (Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.)
  • No advertising frameworks
  • No crash reporting beyond Apple's built-in (which is anonymised and controlled by your iOS Settings)
  • No social login providers
  • No payment processors (the Pro tier is gated client-side only in this version)

3. Permissions

Order requests no system permissions. We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, calendar, health data, or any other sensitive resource.

4. Data Storage & Deletion

Your data lives in the application's sandboxed storage on your device. To delete all your Order data, simply uninstall the app from your iOS device. There is no cloud backup managed by us; if you enable iCloud Backup for your iPhone/iPad, your Order data may be included in that backup under Apple's iCloud terms, which is outside our control.

5. Children's Privacy

Order is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we ever introduce features that change this policy (e.g., optional cloud sync), we will update the date at the top of this page and require explicit user consent before any data leaves your device.
