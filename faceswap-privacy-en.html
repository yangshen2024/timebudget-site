Privacy Policy — FaceSwap

Last updated: May 19, 2026 · App Store ID: TBA · Bundle ID: com.adrianshen.faceswap

1. Overview

FaceSwap ("the App") is an offline-first event-meeting platform built around a 10-minute × 3-round value-exchange mechanism. We respect your privacy and follow a "data not collected" design principle.

2. Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us, our servers, or third parties. All data you enter—nicknames, tags, harvest cards, contact follow-ups—is stored locally on your device using the operating system's local storage facilities.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • No account registration; no email or phone verification
  • No usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

4. Data storage and deletion

Your data lives entirely in your device's local storage (LocalStorage / WKWebView IndexedDB). To delete all data:

  • In-app: Settings → Danger Zone → Clear local data
  • From iOS: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → FaceSwap → Offload App / Delete App

5. Children's privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
